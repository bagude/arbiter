# SPE 162910 on the warehouse — reference report

**Question.** What does SPE 162910 claim about time-rate models for unconventional wells, which of its methods held on the warehouse's wells, and what remains unresolved?

## Observed

- R1. The paper treats the Arps relations as the industry's standard EUR method for more than 80 years (m_f1f0000000aa; page 1).
- R2. An exponential fit over the first 48 months of a NM well gives a monthly decline of 0.03 (m_f1f0000000bb).
- R3. The paper describes the newer time-rate relations as empirical (page 3).

## Interpreted

- R4. The modified hyperbolic model is a hyperbolic branch then an exponential branch with a constant terminal decline (m_f1f0000000cc; page 59). Settled by fitting both branches on one well.
- R6. Monthly rates without pressures limit the warehouse to the rate-time relations (m_f1f0000000cc, m_f1f0000000bb).

## Hypothesis

- R5. The power-law exponential model will describe the first year better than the exponential branch (m_f1f0000000dd). Settled by comparing residuals over months 1-12.

## Unresolved

- Whether TX lease-level and well-level rows give different fitted declines for the same lease.
- Which diagnostic functions can be computed from monthly rates alone.
