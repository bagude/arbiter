Conclusions: 
 
The following conclusions are established from observat ions made in this work:  
 
! The "Db" diagnostic plot should be the primary diagnostic used to establish the well -reservoir character. 
! The "q/Gp" diagnostic plot is an excellent data check, and should be incorporated into diagnostic analyses —  however; 
the expectation of a completely linear trend [ i.e., Duong (2011)] is optimistic. 
! The "!-derivative" diagnostic plot is useful for establishing the  existence of "power-law" flow regimes.
